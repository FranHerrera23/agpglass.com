01

One brief for every supplier.

Define the platforms, intended use and project conditions with qualified specialists. Ask every supplier for the same information. Without a common brief, price comparisons can hide meaningful differences.

02

Distinguish the component from the system.

Identify the manufacturer, reference, configuration, standard and scope of each document. Separate glass evidence from evidence for integration and the vehicle. An unresolved condition is not an implicit approval.

03

Assess ongoing support.

Ask about inspections, replacements, availability and local service terms. Distinguish estimates from contractual commitments. A fleet needs a service plan as well as an initial delivery.

04

Make the decision traceable.

Record the evidence reviewed, accepted conditions and open issues, with accountable owners. A manufacturer’s track record informs the discussion; approval depends on evidence for the specific project.
