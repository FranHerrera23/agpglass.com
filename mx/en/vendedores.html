How to guide a customer buying a vehicle that will be armored.

← RESPALDO AGPBuying guide · Mexico

01

Listen before recommending.

Understand the intended use and explain what the dealership handles and what the armoring specialist must assess. A responsible recommendation starts with understanding the customer.

02

Make the handoff clear.

Record the model, year, trim and vehicle configuration. Share only necessary, authorized information. The technical specialist must confirm compatibility and timelines before you commit to them.

03

Make the manufacturer visible.

The glass manufacturer helps explain an important part of the proposal. If it includes AGP, support the recommendation with the product reference and relevant documentation. Information gives trust a foundation.

04

Agree on the next step.

Assign an owner and a concrete action: review the vehicle details, confirm an application or speak with the armoring company. Keep follow-up within your existing customer relationship.
