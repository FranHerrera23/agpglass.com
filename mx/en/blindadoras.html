In this guide01 / Document what was installed.02 / Make responsibilities clear.03 / Provide useful instructions.04 / Keep information current.
01

Document what was installed.

Collect the accepted proposal, confirmed materials and approved changes. The final record should identify what was actually supplied, with the corresponding documentation.

02

Make responsibilities clear.

Distinguish sales enquiries, usage questions, inspections and warranty matters. The armoring company maintains the customer relationship and coordinates with the manufacturer when needed. Provide real contact channels and confirmed terms.

03

Provide useful instructions.

Share approved instructions for the installed product. Explain where to ask questions and how to report an observation. Manufacturer resources support your service; they do not replace a technical assessment.

04

Keep information current.

Check that data sheets and terms apply to Mexico and the relevant product version. Keep references and service records to support the customer after handover.
