01

Start with your needs.

Tell your armoring company how you intend to use the vehicle. Its assessment should establish the project requirements. A protection level without its standard and scope does not explain what you are buying.

02

Know who makes the glass.

Ask for the manufacturer, product reference and configuration in writing. If you choose AGP, ask for the specific product to appear in the quote. This helps you compare proposals and gives you a reference for future service.

03

Compare the evidence.

Check which standard and test support the product and which configuration they cover. Documentation for glass does not automatically certify the complete vehicle. Confirm who is responsible for integration.

04

Think beyond delivery.

Before taking delivery, ask for care instructions, warranty terms applicable in Mexico and a service contact. Keep the glass identification and installation documents.
